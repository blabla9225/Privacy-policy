This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use this mobile application ("the app").

2. Data Collection and Use

The app itself does not ask for or store personal data that you provide directly. Your game progress, high scores, and settings are stored only locally on your device.

3. Automatically Collected Information

When using the app, certain technical information may be automatically processed by the operating system or by the app distribution platform (such as the Apple App Store). This may include device type, operating system version, language settings, and general usage data. The developer does not use this information to personally identify users.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app is financed through advertising and displays full-screen ads (for example after completing a level). These ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may automatically process technical data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, and general device and usage information.

Depending on your choice in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, ads are either personalized or non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

More information on how Google uses this data is available here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") that permanently disables all advertising. Purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive or store your payment details. Users who purchased the app before it became free keep the ad-free experience automatically at no additional cost.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed in connection with the app. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
